What is Data Science to Me?
To me, data science is a way of using information to better understand patterns, problems, and decisions. It combines things like coding, statistics, and problem-solving, but I think it also involves asking the right questions and thinking carefully about what the data actually represents.

What interests me most about data science is that it can be used in so many different areas. Data can help us understand things like education, healthcare, business, technology, and even social issues. At the same time, I am learning that data is not just numbers. A lot of the time, those numbers represent real people and their experiences, which means data scientists have to be responsible with how they collect, analyze, and use information.
For me, learning data science is not only about becoming better at Python, Excel, or building models. I also want to learn how to take a large amount of information and turn it into something meaningful and useful. I want to understand how to recognize patterns, question results, and explain findings in a way that other people can understand.
As I continue studying data science, I hope to become more confident with the technical side while also understanding the bigger picture behind the data. To me, data science is about using technology and information to solve problems, but also making sure those solutions are thoughtful, fair, and useful in the real world.
